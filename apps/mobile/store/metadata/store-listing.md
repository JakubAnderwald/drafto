## Screenshots

### App Store (iPhone, iPad, Mac) — generated, not committed

Generated by `pnpm store:screenshots:ios` (`apps/mobile`) and `pnpm store:screenshots`
(`apps/desktop`), signed in as the App Review demo account, into each app's gitignored
`fastlane/screenshots/en-US/`, then uploaded with `pnpm store:screenshots:upload:ios` /
`pnpm store:screenshots:upload`. See `docs/operations/builds-and-releases.md` → "App Store
screenshots".

- iPhone 6.9" (1320x2868, iPhone 17 Pro Max simulator) — required
- iPad 13" (2064x2752, iPad Pro 13-inch (M5) simulator) — required (`supportsTablet: true`)
- Mac (2880x1800, 16:10) — at least 1

### Google Play — `../screenshots/`

- `android-tablet-7/` — 7" tablet (1080x1920 min) — optional
- `android-tablet-10/` — 10" tablet (1920x1200 min) — optional
